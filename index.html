Shyam Singha Roy is a 2021 Indian Telugu-language period romantic drama film directed by Rahul Sankrityan. The film stars Nani in a dual role alongside Sai Pallavi and Krithi Shetty in the female leads. The story is based on the theme of reincarnation and takes place partly in Kolkata during the 1970s. It tells the story of Vasu, an aspiring film director who discovers his previous life as Shyam Singha Roy, a Bengali social reformer who fights against social injustice. The movie was released on 24 December 2021.
